Incident Response Runbook

Severity Levels

SEV1 means customer-facing outage affecting more than 20% of users. SEV2 means degraded performance or a partial outage. SEV3 means internal tooling issues.

On-call Escalation

For a SEV1, page the on-call engineer immediately via PagerDuty and open a bridge call within 15 minutes. The incident commander must post updates in #incidents every 30 minutes.

Postmortems

A blameless postmortem is required for every SEV1 and SEV2 within 5 business days.
